How it works

  1. Pick your route

    Choose your college and your bus number once. TrackBus remembers it.

  2. Set your stop

    Select where you board. We calculate the arrival time for that exact stop.

  3. Watch it arrive

    The bus moves on the map in real time. Get a nudge when it’s 10 minutes away.

What you get

Live location

See exactly where the bus is, updated every few seconds.

Arrival time for your stop

Not a guess for the whole route. The time for the stop you use.

Arrival alerts

A notification when your bus is close, so you can stay home a bit longer.

Delay and route notices

Traffic, breakdown or a changed route? The transport office can tell every rider at once.

All your buses in one place

Morning bus, evening bus, exam-day special. Switch between them in a tap.

Light on data

Opens fast on a basic phone and a slow connection.
