# Problem Statement

Students and campus staff often lack timely information about college bus locations and expected arrivals. TrackBus aims to provide a shared, near-real-time view of buses, routes, stops, and estimated arrival times.

## Goals

- Make bus locations easier to access.
- Reduce uncertainty around pickup and arrival times.
- Help administrators identify stale location reports and route deviations.

The project should avoid presenting stale or unverified GPS data as live information.
